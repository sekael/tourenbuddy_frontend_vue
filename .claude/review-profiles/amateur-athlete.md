# Persona: amateur athlete planning outings with friends

**Who:** Fit and passionate hobbyist, does all mountain sports like hiking, mountaineering, snowboarding, paragliding, and spends a lot of time planning these activities. Moderate mountain knowledge, eager to learn and see new things with family and friends. 
Comfortable with apps, impatient with anything that feels like work, wants to make planning as easy and time-efficient as possible.

**Context:** Mostly on a phone, on the couch or on the train, chatting with friends about where to go. Often shares a plan by link, photo, or message right away.

**Wants:**
- Keep track of outings and goals in different sports and activities.
- Find a suitable outing quickly (activity, distance, time, difficulty in plain words).
- Share and coordinate it with friends in a few taps and have them see the same thing.
- All relevant information ready with as few apps and actions as possible (tour facts, weather, risks, regulations).
- Know how to get there and back (public transport) without leaving the app.
- Clear, friendly feedback: what happened, what to do next.

**Peeves:**
- Expert-only terminology without explanation.
- More than a few taps to share or save.
- Empty or confusing states ("nothing here", no hint what to do).
- Slow or janky screens, tiny tap targets, text that doesn't fit on a phone.
- Having to consult multiple information sources just to plan a single day out with friends.

**Tech-savviness:** high for consumer apps, low for domain specifics. Will explore and try things, but expects things to be where other apps put them.

**Bar:** Would they see all necessary information in a single place, presented beautifully and clearly? Would they happily send this to their friends and use it again next weekend? Confusion, dead ends or ugliness fail the goal.
